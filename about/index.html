About TrustWera

Connecting trusted hands with local households and businesses across Kenya.

What TrustWera Is — and What It Is Not

Hiring skilled or casual workers in Kenya has traditionally relied on word-of-mouth, informal WhatsApp groups, or unlicensed middlemen who charge high markups. It is often slow, unpredictable, and difficult to verify someone's reputation.

TrustWera is a community-first worker directory designed to bridge that gap. We make it easy to find reliable local electricians, plumbers, painters, carpenters, mechanics, cleaners, gardeners, and security guards in your specific town or estate.

What We Are

A fast, transparent local directory where clients view verified profiles and contact tradespeople directly with zero platform cut.

What We Are Not

We are not a gig marketplace like Fiverr or Upwork with escrow fees, bidding wars, or platform commissions. You pay workers directly.

How Verification Works

Anyone can register a profile, but not every profile is verified. Look for the green ✓ Verified badge on worker profiles.

When a worker requests verification, our team manually verifies their identity (national ID), confirms their active phone number, checks local references or trade training certificates, and ensures they have a verifiable history in their area before awarding the badge.

Direct Contact & Honest Pricing

TrustWera does not process payments or charge booking fees. When you find a worker you like, you can call them or message them directly on WhatsApp.

You negotiate the scope of work and price directly with the worker. We advise both parties to agree on pricing upfront, inspect work upon completion, and never pay the entire sum in advance.

Built in Kisumu, Built for Kenya

TrustWera was founded in Kisumu with the belief that skilled workers deserve visibility in their home communities, and households deserve peace of mind when inviting someone into their home.

Have feedback, want to partner, or need assistance? Reach out to us directly at hello@trustwera.example.
